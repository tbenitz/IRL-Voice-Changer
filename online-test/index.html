Why some tests are different

Beatrice and RVC: the linked hosted conversion demo supports both formats, but Beatrice may require an actual Beatrice voice model, and RVC may need an RVC model. The online test is not the VCClient desktop program.

Seed-VC and MeanVC2: these hosted demos support source and target/reference audio. Their free queues can have waiting time or go temporarily offline.

LLVC: the research project has official before/after audio comparisons, but I could not verify a publicly hosted LLVC inference service that accepts new recordings. Its tab is explicitly listening-only until a proper hosted backend exists.

Pocket TTS: unlike the other online demos, Pocket runs genuine ONNX inference in your browser. You must choose a voice, then type or dictate text. It generates new speech; it does not preserve exact phrasing and timing like the other VC engines.
