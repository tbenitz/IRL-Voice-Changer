SEPARATE BROWSER DEMONSTRATION

Does Pocket TTS work as a voice changer?

In a turn-based sense: recognize what you said, feed those words into Pocket, and speak the output with a chosen voice. It cannot preserve exact timing like a speech-to-speech VC model.
