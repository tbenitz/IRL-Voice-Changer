What model is this?

The 13.7 MB built-in checkpoints are causal LLVC-style streaming students trained from an RVC teacher, published by HEVoicechanger under GPL-3.0. It is genuine neural speech-to-speech conversion, but it is not an ordinary RVC checkpoint and cannot directly use most RVC voice downloads.

The 13.7 MB model passed a real ONNX Runtime Web WASM test on a Linux CPU. The upstream engine combines it with an optional pitch shifter. Our test with synthesized low-pitched speech produced an input median pitch around 104 Hz and raw ONNX output around 107 Hz—so the pretrained model did not make that sample substantially higher-pitched by itself. Pitch finish is separate DSP, not training. ChromeOS speed and target-speaker resemblance are unverified. The 65 KB Pocket student is not a proven voice clone.
